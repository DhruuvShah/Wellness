Habits & Posture

Morning: Warm water first, breathing exercises, stretch before walking, breakfast within 1.5 hrs.

After Meals: Walk 5-10 min, never lie down within 30 min, sit upright, chew 20-30 times.

Avoid: Sitting over 45 min without moving, crossing legs, low chairs or floor sitting, heavy lifting, flat chappals or heels, very soft mattress.

Sitting: Firm chair, feet flat, cushion behind lower back. Knees at 90 degrees or slightly more.

Standing: Weight on both feet. Don't lock knees. Shift weight every few minutes.

Sleeping: Medium-firm mattress. Back with pillow under knees, or side with pillow between knees.

Exercise & Movement

4 phases — start Phase 1, move up when comfortable (usually 3-4 weeks).

Never do deep squats, jumping, running, or twisting at knees. Protect your prosthetic knees.
Stretching (10 min)

Every morning. Hold 15 sec. Breathe normally. Never bounce.

Neck Rolls

Sit tall. Slowly roll head — ear to shoulder, chin to chest, other ear, look up.

5 each side

Shoulder Shrugs

Lift shoulders to ears, hold 3 sec, drop. Releases neck and upper back tension.

10 times

Seated Side Bend

Raise right arm overhead, lean gently left. Feel the stretch. Switch sides.

15 sec each

Ankle Circles

Lift foot, rotate ankle — 10 clockwise, 10 counter. Switch. Keeps ankles flexible.

10 each way

Hamstring Stretch

Chair edge, extend one leg, heel on floor. Lean from hips gently.

15 sec each leg

Chest Opener

Clasp hands behind back, squeeze shoulder blades, lift slightly.

15 sec x 3
Strength (10 min)

Sturdy chair for support. Go slowly. Stop if pain.

Chair Stand-Ups

Why: Leg strength without deep squatting. Slowly sit then stand. Use armrests if needed.

5 x 2 sets

Seated Leg Lifts

Why: Strengthens thighs supporting your knee. Extend one leg, hold 5 sec, lower slowly.

8 each x 2

Wall Push-Ups

Why: Arm and chest strength without floor. Arm's length from wall, lean in, push back.

8 x 2 sets

Calf Raises

Why: Calves + balance + walking. Hold chair, rise on toes, hold 2 sec, lower.

10 x 2 sets

Yoga, Pranayam & Meditation

Ancient practices for pain relief, calm mind, and flexibility. All knee-safe.

Pranayam (10 min daily)

Anulom Vilom

Why: Balances brain, reduces anxiety, improves lungs.

How: Close right nostril, breathe in left (4 counts). Close left, out right (8 counts). In right, close, out left = 1 round.

10 rounds (5 min)

Bhramari (Bee Breathing)

Why: Calms nerves instantly, relieves headaches.

How: Close eyes, fingers on ear flaps. Deep in. Exhale with humming (mmmm). Feel the vibration.

5 rounds (3 min)

Deep Belly Breathing

Why: Activates relaxation, reduces pain, helps digestion.

How: Hand on chest, hand on belly. In — belly rises. Out — belly falls. Chest stays still.

10 breaths (2 min)
Yoga Asanas

Hold 5-8 breaths. Never force. Chair for support.

Tadasana (Mountain)

Stand feet hip-width, weight equal, arms at sides, palms forward. Lift through crown. Breathe.

30 seconds

Modified Chair Pose

Lower as if sitting but stop midway. Arms forward for balance. Rise slowly. Knees NOT past toes.

5 sec x 5

Cat-Cow (Seated)

Hands on knees. Inhale: arch, look up. Exhale: round, tuck chin. Slowly with breath.

8 rounds

Legs Up (Modified)

Lie near wall, legs resting up. Arms at sides. Close eyes. Just breathe and relax.

5 minutes

Shavasana

Lie on back, legs apart, palms up. Relax each body part toes to head. Let go of all tension.

5 minutes
Meditation (5-10 min daily)

Body Scan

For: Pain relief, relaxation, sleep. Focus from toes upward, saying "relax" at each area. 3 deep breaths to start and finish.

10 min

Gratitude Meditation

For: Positive mindset. Think of 3 things you are thankful for. Feel it. Smile gently. Sit 5 min.

5 min
Nutrition & Meals

Jain vegetarian — no root vegetables, no meat, no eggs. Daily: ~1,350 cal

Foods to Eat
  • Protein: Paneer, tofu, curd, buttermilk, all dals, sprouts, soybean, milk
  • Grains: Wheat roti, bajra, jowar, brown rice, oats, quinoa, poha
  • Veggies: Lauki, turai, kaddu, cabbage, capsicum, beans, bhindi, brinjal, palak, methi, peas
  • Fruits: Apple, papaya, guava, pear, berries, orange, mosambi, banana (1/day)
  • Fats: Almonds, walnuts, flax seeds, chia seeds, ghee (1-2 tsp/day)
  • Anti-inflammatory: Turmeric, ginger, ajwain, jeera, hing, black pepper
  • Calcium: Milk, curd, paneer, sesame seeds, ragi, almonds
Foods to Avoid
  • Root veggies: onion, garlic, potato, carrot, beetroot, radish
  • Refined sugar — use jaggery/dates
  • Maida: naan, bread, pastries, samosa
  • Fried foods, processed/packaged foods
  • Sodas, packaged juices
  • Excessive salt
Hydration

8-10 glasses daily (2-2.5L). Keeps joints lubricated and reduces stiffness.

  • Morning: warm water + lemon
  • 30 min before meals: 1 glass
  • Between meals: sip regularly
  • After exercise: 1-2 glasses
  • Evening: herbal tea (tulsi, ginger, jeera)
  • Avoid large amounts during meals

Tips & Motivation
Slow Progress = Best Progress

Muscles take 4-6 weeks to strengthen. Lasting weight loss: 0.25-0.5 kg/week. Joints need gentle, repeated movement.

A plant doesn't grow faster if you pull it. It grows when you water it consistently.

Promise yourself 15 minutes a day. In 3 months, you'll be a different person.

How Your Body Heals

Prosthetic knees last 15-20 years. Bones have grown around implants. But muscles weakened from less movement — that's the stiffness. These exercises target those muscles. As they strengthen: walking improves, stiffness drops, confidence rises, pain reduces.

Anti-Inflammatory Foods
  • Haldi: Best anti-inflammatory spice. With black pepper for absorption.
  • Adrak: In tea, soups. Reduces muscle pain.
  • Walnuts & Flax: Omega-3 that fights inflammation. Daily.
  • Green Leafy Veggies: Palak, methi. Rich in antioxidants.
  • Amla: Vitamin C powerhouse. 1 daily.
Joint Care — Artificial Knees
  • Move daily — stiffness comes from inactivity
  • 1 kg lost = 4 kg less pressure on knees
  • No floor sitting cross-legged
  • Stairs: Handrails. Strong leg up, weak leg down
  • Good shoes: Cushioned, arch support
  • Ice if swollen after exercise (10 min)
  • Stand every 45 min
Healthy 60-Year-Olds

You're 49. Next 10 years = your power decade:

  • Move daily — walking, stretching, yoga
  • Eat real food — dal, roti, sabzi
  • Sleep 7-8 hrs same time nightly
  • Stay social — family, laughter
  • Manage stress — meditation, pranayam
